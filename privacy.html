Privacy Policy

For Inklings: Journal, Mood & Pet · Last updated: 6 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dong Hung Labs ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with the mobile application Inklings: Journal, Mood & Pet (the "App"), available on Google Play. If you have any questions, contact us at donghunglabs.support@gmail.com.

The short version

Inklings is an offline-first journal. Everything you write — your entries, moods, companion, streaks, display name, and settings — is stored only on your device and is never uploaded to us or anyone else. The only information that ever leaves your device is optional feedback you choose to send us, and technical crash data that helps us keep the App stable. We do not show ads, we do not track you, and we never sell your data.

1. Information that stays on your device

The App is designed to work fully offline. The following is stored only in the App's local storage on your device, and we have no access to it:

  • Your journal entries and their content
  • Your mood check-ins and mood history
  • Your companion (pet), its level, XP, streaks, and outfits
  • Your display name and your chosen writing "energy" (Vibe)
  • App settings, and — if you enable it — your app-lock PIN

This information is not transmitted to us, is not backed up to any cloud by us, and remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. If you use the App's backup feature, a backup file is created on your device and shared only where you choose to send it.

2. Information that leaves your device

Only the following limited information is transmitted off your device, and only in the situations described:

a) Feedback you choose to send

If you use the "Send feedback" feature, we receive what you submit through a Google Form:

  • The feedback type you select (Bug, Idea, or Other)
  • The message you write
  • Your email address — only if you choose to enter it (this field is optional and may be left blank)
  • Basic technical details to help us act on your feedback: the App version, your device model, and your Android version

Feedback is sent to us using Google Forms and stored in Google Sheets. If you provide your email, we use it only to reply to you about your feedback. Sending feedback is entirely voluntary.

b) Crash and diagnostic data

To keep the App stable, we use Firebase Crashlytics (a Google service). If the App crashes or encounters an error, Crashlytics sends us crash reports and basic diagnostic information such as device model, operating-system version, and the technical state of the App at the time of the error. This never includes the content of your journal.

c) Purchases (if you buy Premium)

If a paid Premium option is offered and you choose to buy it, the purchase is handled by Google Play and our purchase-management provider RevenueCat. We receive your purchase status and a purchase identifier so we can unlock your Premium features. We never receive your full payment-card details — those are handled by Google Play.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not show advertisements and do not use advertising identifiers.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your information.
  • We do not use behavioral analytics or tracking to profile you.
  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not read, upload, or transmit the content of your journal.

4. Service providers

The limited data described above is processed on our behalf by:

  • Google — Firebase Crashlytics (crash and diagnostic data), and Google Forms / Google Sheets (feedback). See Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • RevenueCat — purchase management, only if you make a purchase. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

These providers may store and process data on servers located outside your country, including outside Vietnam.

5. Data retention

Information stored on your device remains until you delete it or uninstall the App. Feedback and crash/diagnostic data are retained only for as long as needed to review your feedback and to improve and maintain the App, after which they are deleted or anonymized.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You control the information on your device: you can edit or delete your entries, and uninstalling the App removes its local data from your device.
  • You can choose whether to send feedback, and whether to include your email.
  • To request access to or deletion of feedback or crash data associated with you, contact us at donghunglabs.support@gmail.com.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example, through feedback), contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Because your journal stays on your device, its security depends largely on your device's own protections. We recommend using your device's lock screen and the App's optional app-lock. Data transmitted to our service providers is protected in transit using industry-standard encryption.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes may also be communicated within the App or on its store listing.
